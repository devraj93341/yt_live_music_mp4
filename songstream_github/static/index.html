How to Get Your YouTube Stream Key

Follow these quick steps to find your YouTube Live RTMP stream key:

  1. Open YouTube Studio (studio.youtube.com) in your browser.
  2. Click the "Create" button (top right) and select "Go Live".
  3. Choose "Stream" (the webcam/software encoder tab).
  4. Under "Stream Settings", you will see Stream key (e.g. xxxx-xxxx-xxxx-xxxx-xxxx).
  5. Click "Copy" and paste it into the YouTube Stream Key box in SongStream!

Note: If you have never live streamed before, YouTube requires a one-time 24-hour verification on your channel to enable live streaming.
